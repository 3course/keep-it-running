# Keep It Running — notes for Claude Code

Browser game, Phaser 3 + TypeScript + Vite. See README.md for the loop.

Rules of the codebase:
- `src/sim/` must never import Phaser. It's the whole game and it runs headless (`npm run sim`).
- Every balance number goes in `src/config.ts`. No magic numbers in `Game.ts` or scenes.
- New faults go in `src/data/faults.ts` only: symptom, diagnoses (exactly one `correct: true`), 2+ paths, each path 1–5 steps with minutes and cost. Cheap/slow DIY path first.
- `world` branch: the game is a Three.js world (`src/world3d/`), portrait 390×844, rendered at 1/3 resolution and upscaled nearest-neighbour. It drives `Game` through public methods only — never change `src/sim` or `config.ts` for presentation. The shed layout (0.5 m cells, objects with real heights) is data in `src/world/layout.ts`; world-only state (position in metres, what's carried, the bed, pallets) in `src/world/state.ts`; actions per object in `src/world/interactables.ts`; where each fault's fix happens and each step's one-thumb gesture in `src/world/fixes.ts` (gestures: `src/ui/gesture.ts`; a hands-on `Action` sets `gesture`); presentation numbers (speed, camera, ranges) in `src/world/worldConfig.ts`. All UI is DOM over the canvas (`src/ui/`). The Phaser versions are in history (`003eb00`).
- `src/world/save.ts` serialises every field of `Game`. A new field that isn't plain JSON (a Set, a class, a reference into another array) needs handling there too.
- After any change to config or faults: `npm run sim` and `npm run sim -- --lease`. The economy leans favourable: steady-state competent ~25% margin, sloppy positive but ~8–10 points behind. Over a lease, competent makes rent every time with ~$8k spare, and sloppy makes it ~50% of the time, ending within about ±$3k of the rent (a near miss, not a collapse). The softened downside is in `CONFIG.economy`: `lateGraceMinutes`, `pullAfterDays`, `scrapSalvage`, `walkInPerDay`/`walkInFloor`, `fixCostMult` (set them to 0, or `fixCostMult` to 1, for the old harsh downside).
- Walk-ins (`Game.walkIns`, `CONFIG.economy.walkIn*`): the floor on the day is people. At 1pm, if takings are tracking under `walkInFloor` (pro rata), someone's at the roller door with a small job ("Can you do this today?"); another at 3pm if still under, or the day's regular then if nobody's come; two at most. The ones you serve square up at close for `max(walkInPerDay, walkInFloor − takings)`, shared, so the sim (which serves every one: `servesWalkIns` false) is exactly the old top-up. In the shed (`servesWalkIns` true) they wait `walkInWaitMinutes` for "Sure" / "Not today" (`peopleDay.ts`, a design off the catalogue by `PeopleDay.walkInDesign`; lines `WALK_IN` in `src/people/lines/customers.ts`); turned away or left waiting, their share is lost. "Walk-ins" in the day summary. They don't count toward the best-day milestone. `tests/walkins.test.mjs`.
- Customer pickup (`Game.pickups`, `Game.pallet`, `CONFIG.pickup`): a finished sheet's pay waits on a pickup (one per order; later sheets of the same order ride along). The ute comes `leadMinutes` + seeded 0–`jitterMinutes` after the text, or at the deadline if later ("by close" = `beforeCloseMinutes` before), next morning 8–9 if finished after 3:30 or it would land after close; `slipChance` of them text back running late (+60–90). Money lands at hand-over. Clearing the bed fills the finished pallet (`palletCapacity` 16); full, the bed can't be cleared and the next cut waits (`DayStats.palletBlocked`). The sim hands over the moment the ute's there (`servesPickups` false); in the shed (`servesPickups` true) `w.utePresent` follows `g.pickupsHere`, you carry the stack out, the notepad shows "Texted Sam, picking up ~11:30" (names in `w.pickupNames`), and a waiting ute honks after `honkAfterMinutes`. Rolls use their own seeded hash, not the day's rng. `tests/pickup.test.mjs` (nothing collected within 30 min of completion), `tests/pickup-shed.test.mjs`. Retuned after pickups: a 6-sheet pallet blocked competent ~85 min a day (flying ~10%); at 16 it's ~27 min a day.
- A visiting mate (`Game` distraction `friend`, `src/data/mates.ts`): when he comes up, four answers on him — "Yeah, what's up?" (`src/world3d/mateVisit.ts`: a yarn in the knock-off style, his line over his head, two replies + "Better get back to it", 2–4 turns that know the day, then the offer; words `src/people/lines/visitors.ts`, drunk-spelled once he's had a beer; `Game.yarn`/`holdYarn`/`endYarn`), "Give us a hand?" (`Game.askHelp`: 1–4 jobs, more after a yarn; he walks the pending ones — stack, unload, the old bag, the offcut bin, a sheet onto the bed — via `VisitorWalker.errand`, slower and sillier per beer, `Game.helperTask`/`helperDid`; notepad "Tim: stacking (2 of 3)"), "Grab you a beer?" (you fetch a can; "Give <name> the beer" is on him whenever you're carrying a full one: `Game.giveBeer`, it goes down as his) or before 2pm "Beer after two?" (`Game.beerAfterTwo`: he stays and helps, or he's back at two, `Game.mateReturn`), and "Flat out, sorry". The balance sim never calls any of it unless run with `mate` (`npm run sim -- --lease 200 mate`): competent and sloppy then always yarn, ask for a hand (he does the boards, a truck bundle, the offcut bin, one every `helpMinutesPerTask`) and hand him one beer after two; it prints jobs/visits/beers per lease. Mates come 1–2 times a day (`CONFIG.visitors.perDay`: planned each morning on their own seeded hash, never two arrivals within 90 minutes of the last leaving, at most one after 2pm; the random distraction roll no longer brings them). Measured after that (200 leases, rent $5,600, before the refit to $5,750): without the mate competent ~44% flying and sloppy makes rent ~49%; with him competent spare +~$100, sloppy +2 points. `tests/touch.test.mjs`, `tests/visitor-help.test.mjs`.
- Signing for a delivery is a trace (`src/ui/gesture.ts` `sign`, `WORLD.sign`): a faint signature on the docket, at least 2.5 s, lifting off pauses, a progress line, the driver taps his foot after 5 s, beer wobbles the pen. The pickup hand-over is one tap (no gesture), and the roller door goes up on its own when you carry a stack to a waiting ute.
- The chores (clamps, the bed pile, the offcut bin, the compressor) sit behind `CONFIG.chores` (numbers in `CONFIG.chore`); `npm run sim -- nochores` must reproduce the economy as it was before them. `npm run sim -- seeds=20` for more seeds. See `claude/sim-chores.md`.
- Rare events and milestones sit behind `CONFIG.rareEvents` / `CONFIG.milestones`. With both off (`npm run sim -- baseline`) the sim must reproduce the economy exactly as it was before them. `?event=<id>` (council, bigJob, uteClip) forces one on day 1.
- Browser tests: `npm test` (Playwright, headless Chromium as a touch phone; first time on a machine: `npx playwright install chromium`). They load `?seed=N&intro=0`. `tests/interactables.test.mjs` taps every object's action — a new `case` in `actionsFor` needs a test case there; `tests/touch.test.mjs` taps a visiting mate's labels (before/after 2pm, caps, wave-off and return); `tests/difficulty.test.mjs`, `tests/visitors.test.mjs`, `tests/upgrades.test.mjs`, `tests/overcommit.test.mjs` and `tests/rare-events.test.mjs` check the breakdown ramp, the lesson fire, the visitor rules, each upgrade, the board's-full warning, rare events and milestones on `Game` (no browser). `tests/chores.test.mjs` checks the chores on `Game` and by hand in the shed; `tests/fixes.test.mjs` does each fault fix by hand where it happens (real touch gestures, the beer fumble, the spray aim, the order card, the pickup to the ute); `tapLabel` performs any gesture a label opens. `tests/render-tiers.test.mjs` checks each renderer tier's internal resolution, `?res=`, and which tiers draw the extra detail (textures, sawdust, particles, lighting, outline). Browser tests start the game with `?seed=1`, so runs are repeatable. Run them after changing interactables, labels, the HUD, faults, fires or visitors.
- Designs (`src/designs/`): the shared format for what a job *is* — `Design` (types.ts: id, name, kind formwork/mdf/acrylic, customer, sheet size in mm, material, thickness, colour, `outer` + `inner` SVG path data with `cut`/`engrave`, the customer's note). Twelve in `catalogue.ts` (four per kind; lettering via the stroke font in `font.ts`). `designForJob(job)` picks one by hashing the order (all sheets of one order match) — the sim never knows. `path.ts` flattens path data and builds the toolpath (engrave, holes, outer last) with no DOM or Three.js, so anything later (after-hours tracing) should reuse it. Before: `sketch.ts` (pencil-on-graph-paper SVG) on the job card (`src/ui/jobCard.ts`) and the order card. During: `src/world3d/cutPath.ts` (the head follows the path, kerf ribbons). After: `piece.ts` extrudes the same path, `reveal.ts` plays the <3 s reveal (tap skips; low/bare tiers: no extra light, no particles, plain materials) and renders the gallery thumbnails; `w.made` lists today's pieces for "Made today" in the day summary. A new design must pass `tests/designs.test.mjs` (outline on its sheet, inner paths inside it).
- People (`src/people/`): no overtime any more — at 5 a mate (`friends.ts`: names, `[VOICE NOTES]`, topic stances, after-hours cut ideas; rotated, never the same two days running) is at the roller door: "Grab a beer" or "Head home". The night runs on a headless director (`night.ts`: turns, two replies + "Wind it up", "Another one?", ringing a mate from beer 2, the arc shop → life → deep, a big idea around beer 3, one argument a night rotated by `topicHistory`, an after-hours cut) over dialogue data in `dialogue/` (per-friend files, `bigIdeas.ts`, `topics.ts`, `group.ts`; templates are `TEMPLATE_KEYS`; tag lines with `expr`). `slur.ts` is the seeded drunk-spelling filter. View: `src/world3d/knockoff.ts` (crates by the fridge, now in the dock; two/three-shot; `figures.ts` faces and idles; dusk and the fridge light; `sfx.ts`), `tracing.ts` (the after-hours trace), `peopleDay.ts` (the part-timer `PEOPLE.partTimer`, customers at pickup, next-day gifts), `gratitude.ts` (gifts, keepsakes, the morning's cans and refund bag). Everything persists in `WorldState.people` (`src/people/state.ts`). The only `Game` hooks are `knockOffBeers`, `afterHoursSheet`, `refundCans` (the balance sim never calls them). Content checks: `tests/dialogue.test.mjs` (shape, no real brands, five nights per friend played through); in the shed: `tests/people.test.mjs`.
- Layout (`src/world/layoutPlan.ts`, `src/world3d/layoutEditor.ts`): twelve movable stations; the router, walls, doors and the switchboard are fixed. `problems()` checks a plan, `measure()` walks it with the player's pathfinding, `effectsOf()` turns that into `LayoutEffects` relative to the default plan (numbers in `CONFIG.layout`), so the default layout plays exactly as before. Game adds walking minutes only to walks it times itself (`g.worldWalks` is true in the shed). Saved in `w.layout`. `npm run sim -- layouts [seeds]` compares the presets and flags a spread over 8 points. `tests/layout.test.mjs`.
- Dennis (`src/sim/dennis.ts`, `CONFIG.dennis`): bets as plain data on `Game.dennis` (saved as is), his own seeded rolls, wins as revenue and costs/losses in `DayStats.bets`. The sim turns him off unless run with `-- dennis`, so the default report is unchanged; `npm run sim -- dennis [seeds]` (add `only=<kind>` to price one bet) checks yes-to-all beats no-to-all by 2+ points but its worst seed loses to no's best. View: `src/world3d/dennisView.ts`; lines `src/people/lines/dennis.ts`; he's also a knock-off friend (`occasional`, `lightweight`). `tests/dennis.test.mjs`.
- The rare late one (`src/people/dialogue/lsg.ts`, `src/world3d/lsg.ts`): group nights only, after beer 3, never the first night, once a week; `knockoff.forceLsg` for tests.
- The Lease (`src/world/lease.ts`, `CONFIG.lease`): 20 working days on one seed, rent due at the end. It's a layer round the days (the day sim is untouched; the week-3 wobble uses neutral `Game` hooks: `supplyCostMult`, `callMult`). Weekend screens `src/ui/weekend.ts`, the letter `src/ui/letterCard.ts`, the reckoning in `knockoff.ts` (`reckoning` dep), past leases in localStorage `kir-leases` (the office wall). `npm run sim -- --lease [seeds] [breakdown] [weekly]` reports the rent-made rate, spare, endings, cash percentiles and a rent sweep round `rentDue` (set so sloppy makes it about half the time). Five endings by the spare at day 20 (`src/world/leaseEnding.ts`, thresholds `CONFIG.lease.endings`): flying (Jess goes full time on the spot, Dennis brings a spreadsheet), comfortably, made it, scraped it (up to `graceShort` under the rent counts: the landlord takes what's there and the shortfall rides into the next lease's rent and letter, `PastLease.owed`), didn't. Thresholds are $3k/$7k spare. Each lease is dealt weeks (`src/world/leaseWeeks.ts`, `CONFIG.lease.fortunes`, seeded): quiet weeks first (one in most leases, two in some, never adjacent: calls and the morning board thin out and nobody walks in, so no floor that week — what spreads the competent endings), then up to one of a big order (ten sheets due Friday), a run of rushes, cheap ply, a premium customer. A quiet week is felt, not announced: the radio's long-weekend line, Mai says it's dead (`cafe.ts`), Jess asks if there's anything to do (`JESS.quiet`), Dennis rings every day (`quietWeekCallChance`), and the night offers an after-hours cut whenever it can (`DayFacts.quiet`); `quietWeek(l, g)` in `lease.ts`. They go through neutral `Game` hooks (`setFortune`/`fortuneNow`, `leaseOrder`), so the default sim is untouched; the lease study deals them (`nofortunes` to leave them out, `fortunes` prices each kind per script, `fit` gives the rent at sloppy 45% and the competent split). The playtest baseline (`npm run sim -- --lease 200`, rent $5,750, refitted after the 1–2-a-day mate visits): competent ~19/41/40 made-or-scraped/comfortably/flying, sloppy makes rent ~46% and mostly scrapes it. Note: the walk-in floor is pure margin (more than a competent day's profit), which is why only a quiet week without walk-ins pulls competent down; every other dealt week lifts sloppy too. Words: `src/people/lines/lease.ts`. `tests/lease.test.mjs`.
- The daily (`src/world/daily.ts`, `?daily=1`): the Melbourne date seeds one canonical day; nothing is saved but `kir-daily` (results, streak). Card: `src/ui/dailyCard.ts`.
- Seeds (`src/sim/rng.ts`, `shiftSeed` in `daily.ts`): a new game (and the daily) plays on the Melbourne date as YYYYMMDD (`Shift #20261101` on the day summary and in "Copy day log"); `?seed=N` replays another (a different seed from the save's starts fresh). No `Math.random()` in `src/sim`, `src/world` or anything that changes the game — only in purely cosmetic view code. The world's rolls never share a sequential stream: per-minute rolls (faults, fires, distractions, power trips, truck jitter) are `this.at('<stream>')` (seed + stream + day + minute), the morning's board and calls are `Rng.derive(seed, 'day', dayIndex)`, each order call carries its own `roll` seed (type, customer, sheets, price, deadline, whenever it's answered or rung back), and mates/pickups/rare events/Dennis have their own hashes. Rolls the player causes (misclick, fumble, a clamp left off) are `Game.actionRng`; the sim's scripts flip their coins on their own `P(g)` stream. A new random thing picks one of those, never a shared stream. `npm run sim -- determinism [seed=N]` must say OK (same trace twice; the same mornings, calls and rings for all three scripts); `seed=N` also works with the other sim reports.

- People at the low render resolution (`src/world3d/figures.ts`, `player.ts`, `pixelSnap.ts`): a `Figure`'s legs are a pivot per hip, posed for sitting or standing (straight down, soles on the floor) and kept out of the body, so a lean or laugh never tilts them. Standing still is exactly 0°; the walk is four held poses at 0 / ±`STRIDE` (~18°), never tweened (a few degrees reads as a kink). The player's feet step the same way. Every figure root registers with `snapToPixels`; `snapAll` puts them on whole render pixels just for the render and undoes it after. Speech bubbles (`src/ui/talk.ts` `place`) end a tail and a few pixels above the speaker's head and slide off anyone else's. The cafe's name is DOM lettering over its sign board, up on the upper façade of a two-storey shopfront (in the sky of a portrait shot, above the bubble zone over the regulars' heads); the cafe shot is fitted each frame (`Cafe.FRAME`, `fitShot`) to the camera's field of view and aspect, so portrait phones and landscape desktops both hold the cornice, the sign and everyone. Sparrows on the footpath (`SPARROWS`, `updateSparrows`, cosmetic): held head-dip pecks, a hop each 4–8 s, never two at once and none while a bubble's up (`Talk.speaking`), off for the scene when you walk within 1.5 m; pixel-snapped. A job card that's up when a full scene opens is put away and comes back after, and jobs that land meanwhile wait to be shown. `tests/figures.test.mjs`.
